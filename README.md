## What This Repository Covers

This repository serves as a reference hub for legal resources, CPSC warning documentation, and burn injury claim information related to alcohol-fueled tabletop fire pits, fire bowls, and mini fireplaces.

The Consumer Product Safety Commission documented more than 60 injuries and two deaths linked to these products between 2019 and December 2024. Multiple voluntary recalls and urgent warnings were issued covering brands including Colsen, FLIKRFIRE, Five Below, Rozato, Northlight, and MoonSoll. Unbranded products sold through Amazon and other online platforms are also part of the ongoing investigation.

## The Problem With Alcohol-Fueled Tabletop Fire Pits

Most people who buy a tabletop fire pit do not realize the risk built into how these products work. The fuel is held in an open reservoir that stays exposed during the entire burn cycle. The alcohol flame is often invisible or nearly impossible to see in daylight or normal indoor lighting. When someone leans over to add fuel or check whether the fire is still going, the flame can project outward without warning.

This mechanism is called flame jetting. It is not caused by misuse. It is a predictable result of the open-reservoir design that the majority of these products share.

The CPSC described these products as "extremely dangerous" in December 2024. That language reflects years of incident data that the agency collected before taking action.

## Who May Qualify for a Free Case Review

You may be eligible if all of the following apply to your situation.

- You were injured by a tabletop fire pit, fire bowl, alcohol fire pit, or ethanol fireplace
- Your injuries include second-degree or third-degree burns, permanent scarring, disfigurement, or required any medical treatment
- You can identify the product through a receipt, photos, the product itself, or original packaging
- You suffered the injury in the United States

Time limits apply. Every state has a statute of limitations for personal injury claims, typically between one and four years from the date of the incident. If your incident happened more than a year ago, checking your eligibility now is important.

## Types of Compensation That May Be Available

Compensation in tabletop fire pit product liability cases generally falls into two categories.

**Economic damages** cover the measurable financial losses: emergency room costs, hospitalization, skin graft surgery, physical therapy, follow-up medical care, and lost income during recovery.

**Non-economic damages** cover the personal impact: pain and suffering, permanent scarring and disfigurement, loss of normal daily function, and psychological effects such as anxiety or PTSD following the incident.

Wrongful death claims are also being reviewed for families who lost someone in a tabletop fire pit incident. These claims may cover funeral costs, loss of financial support, and loss of companionship.

All cases are handled on a contingency-fee basis. No fees are charged unless compensation is recovered.

## Evidence to Preserve After a Fire Pit Burn

If you were recently injured, the steps you take in the first hours and days significantly affect your ability to pursue a legal claim. Preserve the following before anything is moved, cleaned, or discarded.

1. The fire pit unit itself, stored in a sealed bag or container
2. Original packaging including model number, brand, and any warning labels
3. Purchase receipt or online order confirmation
4. Medical records from every treatment visit
5. Photographs of your injuries taken at multiple stages of healing
6. Photographs of the scene before anything is cleaned up
7. Contact information for any witnesses who were present
8. All medical bills and out-of-pocket expense receipts
